# Incident Response Policy

## Process
Acme follows a documented incident response process with four phases: detection, containment, eradication and recovery, and post-incident review. The security team is on call 24/7 through PagerDuty.

## Customer notification
In case of a confirmed data breach affecting customer data, affected customers are notified without undue delay and no later than 72 hours after confirmation.

## Post-incident review
A written post-incident review is completed within 5 business days for every severity 1 or severity 2 incident.
